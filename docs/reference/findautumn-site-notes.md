## Product surfaces (No. 01 · Products)

1. **Digital marketing** — "Top spots on Google + Google Hotels, funded by us."
   - Brand defense against the OTAs (OTAs bid on the hotel's own name).
   - "Demand you didn't have before" — high-intent travellers who don't know the hotel.
   - Direct rate beats the OTAs on comparison sites (Google Hotels, Trivago, Kayak).
   - Visible on AI search (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity).
2. **Email marketing** — six lifecycle programs, triggered by guest behaviour, written in the hotel's voice.
3. **Booking engine** — on-brand, three-click checkout, upsells.
4. **Dynamic pricing** — fully automated, adjustable in natural language.
5. **And more** — reputation management, social media, website builder, AI Hotel CoPilot.

## How Autumn works (No. 02) — four stages

ONBOARD (10 min, connect PMS) → RESEARCH (competitors, revenue gaps) →
SET IT (hands off; approve only what you want) → OVERSEE ("Glance at the
highlights. Tweak what matters." Weekly/monthly reports, read on your phone in
two minutes, one sentence makes a change).

The OVERSEE stage is the promise our dashboard has to keep: highlights first,
readable on a phone in two minutes.

## Pricing model (matters for the dashboard)

- Plans: Marketing · Professional (popular) · Convert.
- Marketing plan: hyper-targeted Google Ads, non-brand search + brand name
  protection, Google Hotel Ads, branded retargeting.
- **Pay-for-performance: Autumn covers all ad spend and risk, charging 11–19% on
  bookings directly attributed to that spend.**
- Consequence: the hotel owner's real question is "what did Autumn earn me, net
  of the fee?" The current dashboard never shows the fee. Ours should show the
  fee and the net, because that is what builds trust.
